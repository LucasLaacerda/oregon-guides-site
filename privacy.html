How we measure this page

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how people use our guides page: which buttons they tap, how far they scroll and where they seem to get stuck. Clarity records these interactions, such as clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, and uses cookies to do it. The data is processed by Microsoft. We use it only to make this page clearer. You can read more in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

When you arrive from one of our videos, the web address may include a short video code, such as ?v=12. We pass that code to the checkout so we can see which videos lead to purchases. It identifies the video, not you.

The page also loads its fonts from Google Fonts. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

When you buy a guide

Checkout is handled by Hotmart, our payment partner. Hotmart collects the details you enter at checkout, such as your name, email and payment information, under its own privacy policy. Hotmart shares the order details with us, such as your name and email, so we can deliver your guide and help with any questions.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information.
